# Nexora R&D — Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy (Simulated Document)

## 1. Standard WFH Day
All staff and interns are entitled to work from home every Friday, with working hours
of 8:30am–5:00pm and lunch from 1:00pm–2:00pm, matching normal weekday expectations.

## 2. Ad-Hoc WFH Requests
Employees may request up to 2 additional WFH days per month for personal reasons
(e.g. waiting for a home delivery, medical appointment recovery). Requests must be
submitted via the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance and approved by your
reporting manager.

## 3. Equipment
Employees working from home are expected to use their company-issued laptop and
connect via the Nexora VPN client for access to internal systems. Home internet
costs are not reimbursed under standard WFH arrangements.

## 4. Availability Expectations
Employees working from home must remain reachable via company chat/email during
core hours (10:00am–4:00pm) and attend all scheduled meetings via video call.

## 5. Exceptions
Interns/trainees on their first month of placement are generally expected to be
in-office on WFH Fridays to support onboarding, subject to manager discretion.
