# Nexora R&D — IT Helpdesk FAQ (Simulated Document)

## Q: How do I reset my company account password?
Go to portal.nexorard.com/reset, enter your employee ID, and follow the OTP verification
sent to your registered email. Passwords must be reset every 90 days.

## Q: My laptop won't connect to the office Wi-Fi. What do I do?
1. Forget the "Nexora-Staff" network on your device and reconnect.
2. If that fails, connect via LAN cable at any hot-desk and raise a ticket.
3. For persistent issues, visit the IT Helpdesk counter on Level 2, open 9am–5pm.

## Q: How do I request new software or a license?
Submit a Software Request Form via the IT Service Portal. Standard requests (e.g. Zoom,
Slack, VS Code) are auto-approved within 1 hour. Requests involving paid licenses require
manager approval and may take up to 3 working days.

## Q: I lost my access card. What should I do?
Report the loss immediately to Security (ext. 2200) to deactivate the old card, then
submit a Replacement Access Card Request via the IT Service Portal. A temporary card
will be issued the same day; the replacement typically arrives within 2 working days.

## Q: How do I set up VPN access for remote work?
VPN access is auto-provisioned for all confirmed staff and interns. Download the Nexora
VPN client from the Software Center, log in with your company credentials, and select
the "Cyberjaya-HQ" gateway.

## Q: Who do I contact for hardware issues (broken laptop, monitor, etc.)?
Raise a ticket under "Hardware Support" in the IT Service Portal, or call the Helpdesk
hotline at ext. 2100 for urgent issues.
