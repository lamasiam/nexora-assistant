# Nexora R&D — Leave Policy (Simulated Document)

## 1. Annual Leave
All confirmed full-time employees are entitled to 16 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Interns and trainees are entitled to 1 day of leave per completed month of training.
Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year,
and must be used within the first quarter of that year.

## 2. Medical Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid medical leave per year, supported by a valid
medical certificate (MC) from a registered clinic or hospital. MCs must be submitted to
the HR portal within 2 working days of the leave date.

## 3. Emergency Leave
In cases of family emergencies, employees may apply for up to 3 days of emergency leave
per year. Approval is subject to the employee's reporting manager.

## 4. Application Process
1. Log in to the HR self-service portal.
2. Select "Apply Leave" and choose the leave type.
3. Enter the leave dates and reason.
4. Submit for manager approval — approval typically takes 1–2 working days.

## 5. Public Holidays
Nexora R&D follows the official Malaysian public holiday calendar, published annually
on the HR portal in December of the preceding year.
