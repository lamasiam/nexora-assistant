# Nexora R&D — Expense Claim Policy (Simulated Document)

## 1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business-related expenses,
including local transport for official duties, client/project meeting meals
(capped at RM50 per person), and approved training or conference fees.

## 2. Claim Submission
1. Log in to the Finance Self-Service Portal.
2. Select "New Expense Claim" and attach a clear photo or scan of the original receipt.
3. Fill in the expense category, amount, and business justification.
4. Submit for your manager's approval.

## 3. Submission Deadline
All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted
after this period require additional Finance Director approval and may be rejected.

## 4. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are processed in the next payroll cycle, typically within 2–4 weeks
of manager approval.

## 5. Non-Claimable Items
Personal purchases, fines or penalties, alcohol, and expenses without a valid receipt
are not eligible for reimbursement.
