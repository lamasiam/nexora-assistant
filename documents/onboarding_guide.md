# Nexora R&D — New Joiner Onboarding Guide (Simulated Document)

## Week 1: Getting Set Up
- Report to Human Capital Management on your first day at 8:30am for badge issuance
  and welcome briefing.
- Collect your laptop and access card from IT on Level 2.
- Complete the mandatory Data Protection & Confidentiality e-learning module within
  your first 3 working days.
- Sign and return the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) if not already submitted.

## Week 1–2: Systems Access
- HR system (self-service portal): access granted automatically on Day 1.
- Internal wiki / knowledge base: request access from your reporting manager.
- Project management tool: your manager will add you to the relevant project boards.
- Shared drives: access is role-based; request specific folder access via the IT Portal.

## First Month Checklist
- Meet your assigned buddy/mentor (arranged by your manager within the first week).
- Attend the monthly New Joiners Briefing (HR sends a calendar invite).
- Complete your probation/training goals discussion with your manager by week 3.
- Submit your bank account details and any allowance-related forms to HR before the
  first payroll cutoff date.

## Working Hours
Standard working days are Monday–Thursday, 8:30am–5:30pm, with Fridays as a
work-from-home day (8:30am–5:00pm). Lunch hours are 1:00pm–2:00pm daily.

## Who to Contact
- HR/People & Culture queries: Human Capital Management, ext. 559.
- IT setup issues: IT Helpdesk, ext. 2100.
- Facilities/access card issues: Security, ext. 2200.
